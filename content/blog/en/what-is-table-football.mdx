Table football is a game played on a rectangular table in which players control rows of miniature figures on rotating rods to strike a ball into the opposing goal. It is known as foosball in North America, kicker in Germany, baby foot in France, and calcio balilla in Italy.

## The Basics

A standard table has eight rods. Each rod controls a row of figures: goalkeeper, defenders, midfielders, and forwards. Games are typically played to five or ten goals.

## A Game for Every Space

Table football requires minimal space and no preparation. A game takes between two and fifteen minutes. This is why it works in pub corners, office breakout areas, hotel lobbies, and garden terraces.
