The terms consumer and commercial describe how a foosball table is expected to be used and therefore how it is built.

## Construction Differences

Playing surface: consumer tables use MDF or chipboard. Commercial tables use aluminium composite or stainless steel. Frame: consumer tables are bolted together. Commercial tables are welded. Weight: consumer tables are light. Commercial tables are heavy by design for stability.

## What This Means for Buyers

If a foosball table is going into a hotel, office, restaurant, or bar, a consumer table will need replacement within 1-3 years. A commercial table is a one-time investment.
