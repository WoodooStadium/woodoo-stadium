A designer foosball table occupies an unusual position: it is sports equipment that lives in design-conscious spaces. It needs to play well and look right.

## The Design Leaders

Teckell (Italy): acrylic construction, transparent playing surface. Indoor only. RS Barcelona (Spain): colourful, playful, Bauhaus-influenced. Bonzini (France): industrial heritage, the choice of serious players. Woodoo Stadium (Denmark): stainless steel monocoque, Nordic restraint, permanent outdoor installation.

## The Design Language of the Stadium 11-11

Everything that is not necessary is removed. The form follows the engineering. The material is its own finish. There is no decoration.
