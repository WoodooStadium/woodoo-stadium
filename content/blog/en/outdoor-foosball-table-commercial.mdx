A commercial outdoor foosball table is played more frequently, by more people, in more varied conditions. It needs to look good on day 1,000 as well as day 1.

## What Commercial Use Requires

Volume of play: consumer-grade tables are not built for high-volume commercial use. All-weather installation: commercial outdoor furniture stays outside. Appearance: a scuffed rusting table communicates the wrong thing. Stability: a heavy stable table is safer in public environments.
