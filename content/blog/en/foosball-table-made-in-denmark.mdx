Most foosball tables are manufactured in factories in China, Taiwan, or Eastern Europe. This has implications for buyers investing in a premium object.

## Why Origin Matters

When a table is built in the same city where it is sold, the people who made it are accountable. Northern European manufacturing standards for metalwork are among the highest in the world. Made in Copenhagen communicates craft, restraint, quality, and specificity.

## The Stadium 11-11

Designed and built in Copenhagen, Denmark. TIG-welded frame. Hand-finished surface. Each table inspected before delivery.
