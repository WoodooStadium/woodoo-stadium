Most foosball tables are built for indoors. Put them outside and the playing surface warps, the metal rusts, the paint peels, and the rods seize.

## What Outdoor Actually Means

For a foosball table to be genuinely outdoor-rated, every component must handle rain and moisture, UV exposure, temperature change, and salt air. Most outdoor tables handle one or two of these. Very few handle all four.

## Materials That Work Outdoors

Brushed stainless steel 304 is the standard for serious outdoor equipment. It does not rust under normal conditions, resists UV, handles temperature extremes, and requires no maintenance beyond occasional cleaning.
