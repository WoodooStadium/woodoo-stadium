Handmade is a claim made by many manufacturers and genuinely fulfilled by very few. The meaningful question is whether skilled human judgment was applied at the stages where it matters.

## Where Craft Matters

Welding: a welded stainless steel frame requires skilled TIG welding. Surface finishing: brushing stainless steel to a consistent texture requires skilled manual work. Assembly and calibration: the smoothness of the rods is set during assembly and requires judgment.

## The Stadium 11-11

Built in Copenhagen by a small team. TIG-welded frame. Hand-finished surface. Every table inspected and calibrated before delivery.
