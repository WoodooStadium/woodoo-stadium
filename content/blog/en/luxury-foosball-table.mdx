The word luxury is applied to foosball tables at almost every price point above 500 euros. It means very little without qualification.

## Construction

A luxury foosball table is built differently, not just finished differently. The frame is welded, not bolted. The playing surface is machined flat. The weight reflects the density of the materials.

## The Competitors

The main players in the genuine luxury segment are Teckell (Italy), RS Barcelona (Spain), Bonzini (France), and Woodoo Stadium (Denmark).
