When people search for a custom foosball table, they mean very different things. Some want a logo. Some want specific colours. Some want a fully bespoke object.

## Levels of Customisation

Level 1: Applied graphics - vinyl wraps applied to a standard table. Suitable for temporary activations only. Level 2: Custom colour and finish. Level 3: Integrated brand identity - etched or machined into the surface. Level 4: Bespoke construction.

For a permanent installation in a quality environment, Level 3 or 4 is the correct specification.
