A foosball table in a hotel is not a games room amenity. It is a social infrastructure decision. Done correctly, it extends guest dwell time, creates spontaneous interactions, generates photographs, and differentiates the property.

## What a Hotel Foosball Table Needs to Do

Extend dwell time: guests who play stay longer and order more drinks. Photograph well: every guest who photographs the table creates unpaid content for the property. Handle continuous use: consumer-grade tables need replacement within 1-3 years.

## Placement Considerations

Lobby: high visibility, first impression impact. Bar or restaurant: drives longer stays. Terrace or courtyard: extends the outdoor social offer.
