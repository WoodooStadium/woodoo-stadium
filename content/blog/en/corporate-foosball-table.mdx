The corporate foosball table has had a reputation problem. For a period it became a symbol of forced fun. That era is over. The tables that replaced it are different objects.

## What a Corporate Foosball Table Needs to Do

Create spontaneous social contact. A game takes three to eight minutes. It creates conversation between people in different teams who would not otherwise interact.

Photograph well. The table will appear in employer branding material. It needs to look like a deliberate design choice.

Handle commercial use. An office foosball table is used every day by people who are not careful with it.

## The Stadium 11-11 for Corporate Spaces

150 kilograms. Brushed stainless steel. Optional brand identity integration.
