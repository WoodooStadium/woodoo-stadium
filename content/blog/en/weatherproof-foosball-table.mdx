When a manufacturer calls a foosball table weatherproof, it means very little. The term is not regulated, not standardised, and not tested to any common benchmark.

## What to Check Instead

Playing surface: MDF and wood are not weatherproof regardless of treatment. Look for aluminium composite, solid polymer, or stainless steel. Frame: powder-coated steel fails within 1-2 seasons outdoors. Stainless steel 304 is the correct material.
