The snake shot is a foosball technique in which the player pins the ball under the toe of a forward figure, then uses a rolling wrist motion to release and shoot in a single motion. Executed correctly it is extremely fast.

## How the Shot Works

The ball is pinned under the front figure on the three-rod. The player holds the rod loosely and uses a rolling motion to whip the rod through the shot. The ball releases in under 100 milliseconds. The key is the loose grip.

## Why It Is Controversial

The snake shot technically skirts the ITSF no-spinning rule. Some regional associations have banned it. The ITSF permits it at international level.

## Why Table Quality Matters

The snake shot requires smooth predictable rod mechanics. The Stadium 11-11 is built with calibrated rods and precision bearings so that technique determines the outcome.
