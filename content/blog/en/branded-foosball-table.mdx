A foosball table with your company logo is not a novelty. Done correctly, it is one of the most effective pieces of physical brand communication available.

## Why Branded Objects Work

Branding on a passive surface is seen once and forgotten. Branding on an object that people interact with, photograph, and talk about is seen repeatedly by people who are engaged and positive.

## What Branded Means on a Foosball Table

Etched and lit brand plates: the brand identity is etched into the stainless steel body and lit from within. Part of the object, not applied to it. Custom colour and finish. Custom figures. Custom playing surface.
