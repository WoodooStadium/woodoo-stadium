The history of foosball begins depending on who you ask in England, France, Spain, or Germany. The invention is contested. The patents are old.

## The First Patent

The first documented patent for a table football game was filed in the United Kingdom in 1921 by Harold Searles Thornton. His design is recognisably the game played today.

## The Spread Across Europe

The game spread rapidly through Europe in the 1940s and 1950s. By the 1970s it had become a competitive sport in the United States. The International Table Soccer Federation was founded in 2002 and now oversees tournaments in over 70 countries.
