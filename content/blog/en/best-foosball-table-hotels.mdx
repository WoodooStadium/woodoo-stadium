Hotels buying a foosball table are navigating a market from 500 euro consumer tables to 30,000 euro bespoke installations.

## Category 1: Budget Hospitality

Brands: Garlando, Warrior, Carrom. Suitable for budget hotels and sports bars. Durability under commercial use: 1-3 years.

## Category 2: Mid-Range Commercial

Brands: Bonzini B60, Leonhart Hurricane, Roberto Sport. Suitable for quality bars and boutique hotels. Durability: 5-10 years with maintenance.

## Category 3: Premium Commercial

Brands: Teckell, RS Barcelona, Woodoo Stadium. Suitable for luxury hotels and corporate headquarters. Of these only the Woodoo Stadium 11-11 is designed for permanent outdoor installation.
