The short answer: most cannot. The long answer depends on what the table is made of.

## Why Standard Tables Fail Outside

MDF absorbs moisture and swells. Paint peels at joints. Chrome pits in salt air. Chipboard disintegrates when wet.

## What Permanent Outdoor Use Requires

Stainless steel or marine-grade aluminium for all structural elements. Sealed playing surface. Stainless rods. No wood, MDF, or chipboard anywhere in the construction.

## The Stadium 11-11

Designed specifically for permanent outdoor installation. Welded stainless steel 304 monocoque construction. No seasonal storage. No covers. No maintenance beyond cleaning.
