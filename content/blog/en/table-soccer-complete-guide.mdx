Table soccer is the North American term for the game known as table football in the UK and Europe, and foosball almost everywhere else. The name changes by country; the game does not.

## What Is Table Soccer?

Players control rows of miniature figures mounted on rods across a rectangular table, striking a ball into the opposing goal. Games are typically played to five goals.

## What Separates a Good Table

The playing surface must be flat and consistent. The rods must slide and rotate without resistance. The ball must roll true. Cheaper tables compromise all four.
