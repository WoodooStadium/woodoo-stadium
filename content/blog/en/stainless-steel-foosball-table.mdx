Most foosball tables are built from MDF, chipboard, and chrome-plated steel. These materials are cheap to manufacture, adequate indoors, and unsuitable for any environment involving moisture or extended use.

## What Stainless Steel Changes

Stainless steel 304 does not corrode under normal conditions. It does not warp, crack, or degrade with temperature change. It is the only material suitable for permanent outdoor installation without additional treatment.

Brushed stainless steel ages without degrading. Scratches become part of the surface rather than damage to it.
