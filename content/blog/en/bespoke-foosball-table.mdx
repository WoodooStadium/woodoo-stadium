Bespoke means made to order, to specification, for one client. A bespoke foosball table is an object that does not exist before the brief.

## Who Orders a Bespoke Foosball Table

Bespoke is appropriate when standard dimensions do not fit the space, when the required material is not available in a standard product, or when the client wants something that does not exist anywhere else.

Lead time for a genuinely bespoke piece is typically 12-20 weeks from brief to delivery.
