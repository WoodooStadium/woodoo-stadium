Brand activations live and die on one metric: how long does someone stay and how much do they engage? A well-executed foosball activation outperforms most alternatives on both.

## Why Foosball Works as Activation

It creates competition and crowds. A foosball match draws spectators who become participants. It creates photographs. A branded well-made table is photogenic. It is inclusive and accessible to almost everyone.

## What to Specify

Brand identity integrated not applied. Heavy enough to feel permanent. Excellent playing surface. Genuinely outdoor-rated if the event is outside.
